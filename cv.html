Research Experience

  • 2024 – Present

    Graduate Research Assistant

    University of Wisconsin–Madison · Advisors: Victor M. Zavala and Styliani Avraamidou

    • Developed a multi-scale MILP framework coupling demand-response operation of electrolyzers in day-ahead and real-time markets with usage-based degradation and stack replacement scheduling.
    • Designed an aggregate-informed Benders decomposition algorithm that cuts final optimality gaps from 85% to under 5% on 40-year instances with up to 5.6M binary variables, where monolithic solves run out of memory.
    • Building PlasmoSurrogates.jl, a Julia framework on Plasmo.jl for decomposing optimization models that combine ML surrogates with algebraic models.
