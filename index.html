Kiernan Jennings

PhD Candidate, Chemical & Biological Engineering
University of Wisconsin–Madison

I develop models and algorithms for large-scale optimization problems, working at the intersection of mathematical optimization and machine learning. My research applies these methods to energy systems, with a focus on reliability, sustainability, and equity.

I am co-advised by Victor Zavala and Styliani Avraamidou. Much of my work centers on surrogate-based and distributed methods that make computationally demanding problems tractable at scale.

Research Curriculum Vitae

Focus Areas

Large-Scale Optimization

Decomposition and distributed algorithms for structured problems that are intractable to solve monolithically.

Surrogate Modeling

Machine learning approximations embedded in optimization formulations to reduce the cost of repeated model evaluation.

Energy Systems

Planning and operations problems in power and energy networks, evaluated against reliability, sustainability, and equity objectives.
