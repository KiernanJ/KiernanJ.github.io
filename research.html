Research

Large-scale optimization and decomposition algorithms, with applications in energy systems.

Overview

My research develops optimization models and decomposition algorithms for energy systems where decisions span across multiple different time scales. The central application is power grid integrated electrolysis and deciding how electrolyzers can provide flexibility to the grid. These problems require new system models and novel mathematical techniques to solve these problems.
